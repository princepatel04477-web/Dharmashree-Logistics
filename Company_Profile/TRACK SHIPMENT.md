# **TRACK SHIPMENT** 

## TRACK YOUR SHIPMENT. STAY INFORMED AT EVERY STEP. 

With Dharmashree Logistics, keeping track of your shipment is simple. Enter your AWB or tracking number to view the latest available shipment-status information and stay updated on its journey. Whether you are a business sending regular orders, a customer waiting for a parcel or a partner managing freight movement, shipment visibility helps you plan better and communicate with confidence. If you need assistance, our support team is available to help. 

## EASY AWB / TRACKING LOOKUP 

Every shipment is assigned a unique Air Waybill (AWB) or tracking number. You can enter this number on the Dharmashree Logistics tracking page to search for your consignment. 

The tracking page is designed to make shipment lookup quick and convenient. Simply enter the number carefully and select “Track Shipment” to access the latest available information. This helps you check shipment details without having to call for every update. 

## LATEST SHIPMENT STATUS 

Tracking information helps you understand the current stage of your shipment. Depending on the shipment journey, the status may show that it has been booked, picked up, in transit, arrived at a facility, out for delivery, delivered or requires attention. 

These updates help businesses manage customer expectations, prepare for delivery and coordinate with receiving teams. While updates reflect the latest available operational information, actual delivery times may vary due to route conditions, weather, security checks, address availability or other factors outside normal control. 

## BOOKING AND INVOICE REFERENCE SUPPORT 

If you do not have an AWB or tracking number, your booking reference or invoice reference may help our support team locate the shipment details. Keep relevant booking information ready when contacting us, including the sender name, receiver name, pickup date and destination. 

For business customers managing multiple consignments, booking and invoice references can make it easier to identify a specific shipment and receive the right assistance from the support team. 

## DEDICATED HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT 

Sometimes you may need more help than a tracking update. If your shipment status is unclear, you need to correct delivery information, have a booking query or require support with a delayed consignment, our team is here to assist. 

Please share your AWB or tracking number along with your query when contacting support. This helps us understand your requirement faster and provide more relevant guidance. 

## TRACK WITH CONFIDENCE 

Dharmashree Logistics is committed to providing clearer visibility and dependable support throughout the delivery journey. Use the tracking page to stay updated, and contact our support team whenever you need help with your shipment.
