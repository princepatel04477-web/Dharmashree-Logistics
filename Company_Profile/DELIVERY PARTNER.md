# **DELIVERY PARTNER** 

## GIVE YOUR HARD WORK A NEW DIRECTION. 

Join the Dharmashree Logistics delivery partner network and become part of a growing logistics community. We offer flexible earning opportunities for individuals who want to use their time, commitment and delivery experience to support businesses and customers. Whether you are looking for a regular delivery opportunity or flexible work that fits around your schedule, becoming a delivery partner can help you build a dependable source of income while contributing to reliable movement across your city. 

## FLEXIBLE WORK OPPORTUNITIES 

Every delivery partner has different availability and personal commitments. Dharmashree Logistics aims to offer opportunities that provide greater flexibility in how you take up delivery work. Depending on operational requirements and service availability, you can take delivery assignments that suit your preferred working time and location. This flexibility can be useful for individuals looking to work independently, earn through delivery services or add a productive opportunity alongside other commitments. 

## REGULAR DELIVERY ASSIGNMENTS 

Reliable work opportunities are important for delivery partners. Through our growing logistics network, we aim to connect eligible partners with regular delivery assignments based on local demand and operational requirements. 

These assignments may include documents, parcels, e-commerce orders, business shipments and local deliveries. Regular delivery opportunities can help partners plan their work more confidently while contributing to timely and dependable service for customers. 

## SUPPORTIVE ONBOARDING PROCESS 

Starting a new delivery partnership should be simple and clear. Dharmashree Logistics supports eligible partners through an onboarding process that helps them understand the requirements, documentation and basic operating procedures. 

Our team aims to make the joining experience smooth by providing clear guidance at every important stage. Once onboarded, delivery partners can receive the information needed to begin handling assignments responsibly and professionally. 

## NEW OPPORTUNITIES FOR GROWTH 

Becoming a delivery partner is more than just completing deliveries. It is an opportunity to build experience, develop professional discipline and become part of a network that grows with the demand for reliable logistics services. 

As the Dharmashree Logistics network expands, new opportunities may become available for committed delivery partners. Consistent performance, reliability and customer-focused service can help partners build a stronger working relationship with the network over time.
