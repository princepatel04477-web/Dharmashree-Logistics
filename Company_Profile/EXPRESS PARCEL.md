# **DOORSTEP PICKUP FOR BUSINESS SHIPMENTS** 

You do not need to take your shipments to a drop-off point. Dharmashree Logistics provides doorstep pickup from your business location, making dispatch more convenient and time-efficient. You can prepare parcels at your office, warehouse, store or fulfilment centre while our pickup coordination team supports the collection process. This service helps businesses manage regular dispatches, reduce operational effort and process customer orders more quickly. 

# **RELIABLE INTERCITY AND REGIONAL DELIVERY** 

Every business has different delivery requirements. Some shipments need to reach nearby cities, while others need reliable regional or intercity movement. Our Express Parcel service provides planned, dependable delivery support so you can offer a better experience to customers and business partners.
