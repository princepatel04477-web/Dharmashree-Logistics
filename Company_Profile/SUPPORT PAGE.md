# **SUPPORT & FAQ** 

## YOUR QUESTION IS OUR PRIORITY. 

For help with shipping, tracking, pickup, freight or any other service-related requirement, connect with the Dharmashree Logistics support team. We are here to provide clear guidance and help you understand the next steps for your shipment. 

To help us assist you faster, please keep your AWB or tracking number, booking reference, sender and receiver details, and shipment date ready when contacting support. 

## HOW DO I TRACK MY SHIPMENT? 

You can track your shipment by entering its AWB or tracking number on the Dharmashree Logistics tracking page. The tracking page will show the latest available shipment-status information. Your shipment status may indicate that the consignment has been booked, picked up, is in transit, has arrived at a facility, is out for delivery or has been delivered. If you cannot find your shipment or the status requires clarification, contact our support team with your tracking number. 

## HOW DO I RAISE A PICKUP REQUEST? 

To request a pickup, contact Dharmashree Logistics through the pickup-request form, telephone number or official business contact channel. Please provide the pickup address, contact person, mobile number, shipment type, approximate weight or dimensions, number of parcels and destination details. 

Our team will review the request and coordinate the available pickup option. Pickup availability, timing and serviceability may depend on the location, shipment requirement and operating schedule. 

## HOW LONG WILL DELIVERY TAKE? 

Delivery time depends on the pickup and destination locations, selected service, shipment type, route conditions and operational requirements. Local deliveries may be completed on the same day or the next working day where service is available, while regional and intercity shipments may take longer. The estimated delivery timeline shared during booking is an expected timeframe and not a guaranteed delivery commitment unless specifically agreed in writing. You can use your AWB number to track the shipment’s latest available status. 

## WHAT SHOULD I DO IF MY SHIPMENT IS DELAYED? 

If your shipment is delayed, first check its latest status using the tracking page. Some delays can occur due to weather conditions, traffic restrictions, operational constraints, incomplete address details, recipient unavailability, security checks or other circumstances outside normal control. If you need further assistance, contact our support team with the AWB or tracking number. We will review the available shipment information and guide you on the next steps. 

## WHAT SHOULD I DO IF MY SHIPMENT IS DAMAGED? 

If a shipment is received in a damaged condition, please inform Dharmashree Logistics as soon as possible. Keep the shipment, packaging, invoice and any relevant photographs available for review. Our team may require the AWB number, booking details, description of the issue and supporting images or documents. Damage-related queries are reviewed according to the shipment booking terms, declared value, packaging condition and applicable service policies. 

## NEED MORE HELP? 

For any query related to shipment booking, pickup, tracking, delivery, freight, billing or returns, our support team is ready to assist. Share your shipment reference and a clear description of your concern so that we can provide the most relevant support.
